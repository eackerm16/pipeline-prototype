# Pipeline

An AI recruiting copilot for business school career centres.

Career centres have a few advisors for thousands of students and no visibility into who's struggling until recruiting is over. Pipeline gives each group what it's missing:

- **Students**: one feed of CPG and consulting roles, a fit check against any posting (paste or upload a PDF resume), an application tracker with calendar reminders, and interview drills for the specific role.
- **Advisors**: the whole cohort's pipeline in one view, with risk flags, a suggested next step for each student, and CSV export.
- **Directors**: a recruiting funnel and placement data collected automatically as students work, with no grad surveys.

## Status

v1 prototype. Static site with sample data. Fit scoring is keyword-based for now.
